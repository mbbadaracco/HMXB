One reference of F23 that is gone
---------------------------------
F23 cites the preprint 2015arXiv150301087G for six quantities of
AX J1841.0-0536 and IGR J18450-0435. It was withdrawn from arXiv and is no longer
retrievable, so those six values were discarded rather than carried on trust.
What replaces them is read from two papers that are available, both cited above:
Goossens et al. (2013) for the 5.72 d period of AX J1845.0-0433, the other name of
IGR J18450-0435, and Bozzo et al. (2024) for AX J1841.0-0536.
